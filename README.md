## 🎯 Profile summary

I'm a solution architect and engineering leader who takes complex, high-stakes domains from first principles to production. I design the architecture, model the domain, build the systems hands-on, and lead the teams that run them at scale.

My range is my edge. Over 10+ years I've delivered platforms end to end across payments and logistics and supply chain, which taught me to learn a new domain quickly, find where the real complexity lives, and build systems that stay correct under load. In payments, that means secure, high-throughput systems processing millions of transactions daily, from ISO 8583 protocol engines and EMV card processing to UPI infrastructure and soundbox platforms.

I grew from intern to Engineering Manager at one company. Today I lead 20+ engineers, set architecture standards, mentor senior engineers, and turn demanding requirements into compliant, reliable systems that run at 99.99% uptime.

## 🧠 How I work

- **Model the domain first.** Good architecture starts with understanding the business, not the framework.
- **Design for failure and scale.** Systems should stay correct under load and degrade gracefully.
- **Security and compliance by default.** Built in from day one, not bolted on at audit time.
- **Grow engineers, not just systems.** Teams that outlast the code they write.
